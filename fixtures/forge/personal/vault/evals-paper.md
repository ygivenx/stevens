# Containment is not resolution

Support-agent benchmarks overstate deflection: most count containment without checking resolution.

The paper defines containment as no human reply within 24 hours and resolution as the issue fixed with no reopen in seven days. Across eleven vendors, contained tickets reopened 18 to 26% of the time, and another 10 to 15% went silent with no CSAT either way. The headline deflection numbers vendors publish are containment numbers.

## The definition to demand

Ask for three numbers, not one: contained share, reopened-in-7d share, and resolved-without-reopen share. A 41% deflection figure with no reopen rate attached is a containment figure wearing a resolution costume. The paper's rule of thumb: discount published deflection by a third until the vendor shows reopen data.

## Why this matters for Compass

The pilot counts containment exactly the way the paper warns about: no human reply in 24 hours. Ivy flagged the same gap in the eval setup. If the GA review opens with the 41%, open with the definition, then ask for the reopen rate before reacting. See also [[Evals talk notes]] for the conference version of the same argument.
