# Evals talk notes

The evals talk argued deflection is the wrong headline metric: containment without resolution flatters every vendor presenting.

Ninety seconds of Q&A mattered more than the slides. Someone asked whether the 70% deflection figure on screen counted tickets that went silent, and the speaker said yes, silence counts as containment in every benchmark they publish. The room laughed in the way rooms laugh when they already knew.

## Numbers worth keeping

Contained tickets reopen about a fifth of the time within a week. CSAT is measured on contained tickets only, so the tickets that reopen never get surveyed. Nobody presenting would give a resolved-without-reopen number, which is itself the number.

## Takeaway

[[Containment is not resolution]] has the paper version. For any ship review: ask what share of contained tickets stayed resolved, and who owns the ones that came back.
