# NDR vs logo churn

Reading note. NDR and logo churn answer different questions and decks love quoting the flattering one.

## The math

Take 100 customers paying $1 each. Seven leave (7% logo churn), the survivors expand to $1.25 on average. Ending revenue is $116 on a $100 base: 116% NDR, while nearly one in ten logos walked out. Add one noisy quarter of price increases and you get 118% NDR next to 1.8% net churn with the same 7% logo churn underneath. All three numbers can be true at once.

## Why it matters for Helios

The deck's 1.8% net churn and 118% NDR tell us expansion is working. They say nothing about how many logos leave, which is what determines whether the go-to-market engine has to refill the bucket every year. A company can grow 2x into its Series A on expansion and still have a leaky base.

## Questions this raises

- Gross retention, trailing twelve months, no adjustments.
- Logo churn split by cohort year: is it improving as the product matures?
- Expansion concentrated in a few accounts, or broad-based?
