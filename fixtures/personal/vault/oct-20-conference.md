# Oct 20 conference notes

Notes on the mid-market SaaS churn benchmarks talk, Oct 20. 90 seconds of Q&A at the end mattered more than the slides.

## The sample

140 private SaaS companies, $5–50M ARR, FY2025 numbers, self-reported with investor verification on about half. Mid-market defined as 200–2000 employees at the customer. The speaker works at the benchmarking firm full time; this is their flagship dataset, not a vendor survey.

## The numbers

- Logo churn clusters at 6–8% a year. Median 7.1%. Nobody in the sample holds under 5% without annual prepays plus an embedded workflow.
- Net revenue churn medians 3.1% after expansion. The gap between logo and net is the whole story: expansion dollars hide departing logos.
- Top quartile keeps gross retention above 90% by selling multi-year terms into operations teams, where switching costs are highest.

## The 2% rule

Anyone quoting under two percent is measuring something else. Usually one of three things: net revenue churn quoted without the word net, a single trailing quarter annualized, or logo churn computed after removing downgrades and contractions. Ask which one before believing it.

## Q&A

One audience question, worth writing down: does the 6–8% include downgrades, or logo churn only? Answer: logo churn only. Net revenue churn with expansion is a different story, and decks that blend the two in one chart should be read with suspicion.

## Takeaway

[[Helios prep]]: the deck says net churn 1.8%. Against this sample that number can only be net of expansion, and aggressive expansion at that. Go in asking for the logo number and the definition. See also [[NDR vs logo churn]] for the math.
