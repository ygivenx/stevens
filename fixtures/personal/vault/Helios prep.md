# Helios prep

Second meeting, Oct 29. Helios is a mid-market workflow SaaS company raising a $12M Series A. CEO Dana Reyes, CFO Priya Nair. First meeting Oct 21 covered product and go-to-market; this one is about the numbers.

## What the deck claims

Net churn 1.8%, NDR 118%. No logo churn figure anywhere in the deck. Gross retention mentioned once, without a number. The appendix has a cohort chart but the axes start in 2025, so there is one year of history.

## Open questions

1. What is logo churn, trailing twelve months, with downgrades counted as churn? This is the number to get first, before any discussion of expansion.
2. How much of the 118% NDR comes from seat expansion versus price increases? If it is mostly price, the expansion story is thinner than it looks.
3. What share of the base is on annual versus monthly terms? Monthly-heavy bases churn faster and the sample says so.
4. Which customer segment churns most, by size and by use case? If the churn concentrates in one segment, the rest of the base may be fine.

## Plan

Open with the benchmark: category logo churn runs 6–8% a year per [[Oct 20 conference notes]], so a 1.8% net figure needs its definition on the table. Let the CFO define it before reacting. Then walk the four questions in order and write down exact numbers, not adjectives.
