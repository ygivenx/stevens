# M-2417 tumor board prep

M-2417 goes to the tumor board Thursday: IIIB lung adenocarcinoma, EGFR exon 19 deletion, PD-L1 40%.

The chart says start standard chemo-immunotherapy next week. The question I want answered first is whether the EGFR result changes the plan, because the molecular tumor board meets in nine days and they tend to favor targeted combinations for exactly this marker profile. Starting now may close the trial option, which needs treatment-naive patients.

## What to open with

Ask whether anyone has seen response data for EGFR-mutant IIIB on a targeted combination versus the standard regimen. The Nature highlight from yesterday ([[EGFR combinations]]) has the numbers. Then ask who owns trial eligibility screening before the board votes.
