Stevens

Thesis

Future knowledge workers won't mostly write; they'll read, listen and absorb. Stevens takes in what you learn off the clock — talks, readings, conference captures from Ray-Ban Meta glasses — and connects it to the work decision in front of you, with a source on every sentence.

Stevens is named after the butler in The Remains of the Day: he knows the bigger picture, prepares you before the delegation arrives, and is discreet about everything. This is not RAG over notes. The new parts are the bridge (off-the-clock learning → work decision) and the enforced, inspectable boundary.

The demo persona is a VC Associate at a fictional firm; the pitch generalizes to an engineer reading papers before a design review.

The 3-minute demo

  1. Hook. One line of the thesis.
  2. Capture. Last week's conference as real Ray-Ban Meta POV footage: talk audio → live Voice Transcribe with speaker labels and timestamps; glasses photo of a slide → live Spark vision. Both land in the personal vault.
  3. Unprompted brief. For tomorrow's second Helios meeting. The bridge: "Deck: net churn 1.8% (Enterprise · Fact) — at the Oct 20 talk, Speaker 2 at 14:32: 'mid-market churn in this category runs 6–8%' (Personal · Fact) → ask how they define churn (Inference)." Chip: 1 quote crossed personal → work.
  4. Associate asks "Should we take the second meeting?" The answer comes from visible data, plus: Restricted information material to this decision exists; escalation drafted to a Partner.
  5. Boundary inspector. For each model call: what went in, what was withheld and why. The partner memo is absent from the answer call, 1 of N personal items crossed, and web search was off for every enterprise call.
  6. Switch to Partner. The memo shows (Helios competes with portfolio company Lumen); the Partner approves "take the meeting after conflict review"; the decision is logged.
  7. Close. "Same Stevens for the engineer before a design review." Tagline.

Two boundaries, one mechanism

Personal ↔ work

You own your personal vault (talks, readings, conference captures); the firm owns the enterprise vault. Stevens reasons across both and owns neither. Only a cited quote crosses from personal to work. Nothing crosses from work to personal.

Agent ↔ viewer

Stevens may know more than you are allowed to see. Restricted data never enters the prompt that writes your answer. A separate check can only say "restricted information material to this decision exists" and draft an escalation.

Both boundaries are enforced in code: one context assembler builds every model call and records what went in and what was withheld, and the boundary inspector shows it. A weak model is never asked to keep a secret.

What is real vs simulated

  • Real: the Meta model calls through the API, and the Ray-Ban Meta captures — pre-recorded, not streamed.
  • Simulated and labeled: the VC firm and its data (fictional), the staged talk, and permission scopes modeled on Meta's Personal Agent Protocol. Every simulated surface carries a Simulated — <reason> badge; the real call is always the primary path.
